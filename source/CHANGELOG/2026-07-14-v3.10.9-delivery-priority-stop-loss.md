# 2026-07-14 | ae-sdd v3.10.9 - delivery priority and stop-loss policy

## Scope

This change records execution-discipline rules discovered while delivering Story-004. It does not change gates, scanners, business code, or POM behavior.

## Policy

- Feature Story code and focused tests take priority over framework repair.
- A framework defect exceeding 15 minutes or two repair/review rounds becomes an independent work item.
- VerificationPlan/evidence is created only after code, tests, and delivery documents are frozen.
- Repeated tests require a relevant hash change or an explicit recorded risk reason.
- Focused tests precede at most one affected-module regression; full-suite runs require shared infrastructure changes.
- Agents have a two-minute evidence deadline and at most one reassignment; review has at most two rounds.
- Explicit user rules override legacy documentation; status reports must cite evidence.
- Business code, POMs, and baselines must never be changed merely to satisfy a gate.
